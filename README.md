# SME Financial Health Assessment Platform

## 🚀 Overview
The SME Financial Health Assessment Platform is a full-stack, AI-powered web application designed to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) understand their financial position quickly and clearly. Business owners can upload financial data and receive actionable insights, visual dashboards, and a financial health score without requiring deep accounting knowledge.

The platform follows a **SaaS credit-based model**, where new users receive free credits and are prompted to subscribe for additional usage.

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement
SMEs often lack access to affordable financial advisory tools and struggle to interpret financial statements. This platform bridges that gap by providing automated analysis, risk identification, and visual insights using AI-driven logic.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- Business user registration & login
- Free onboarding credits for new users
- Credit-based financial analysis system
- Automated financial health scoring
- Revenue, expense, profit & cash flow analysis
- Visual dashboards with bar charts
- Risk identification & optimization insights
- Subscription plans (mock implementation)
- Responsive UI for desktop & mobile users

---

## 📊 Financial Metrics Analyzed
- Total Revenue
- Total Expenses
- Profit
- Cash Flow
- Expense Ratio
- Financial Health Score (0–100)
